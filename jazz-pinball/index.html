How it plays

Pick your set. Before launching, flip the flippers to choose one of five jazz standards. Its featured table element scores 2x, and racking up enough of them completes the Ovation Challenge.

Light the Solo. Complete the challenge and the spotlight scoop lights up. Sink it to face 3 questions from that era of jazz history — get 2 right to complete the set, raise your multiplier, and bank 500K. Miss, and the ovation resets.

After Hours Multiball. Complete the top lanes to light LOCK, then sink the scoop to lock a ball. Two locks, then one more scoop shot starts 3-ball multiball — orbits score 350K jackpots.

Bebop Frenzy. Spell B-E-B-O-P on the roundel targets for a 2-ball frenzy — targets pay 25K each while it runs.

Encore. Drain a ball and the crowd offers one trivia question per ball to win it back. Complete all five sets for an extra ball and 2.5M.
